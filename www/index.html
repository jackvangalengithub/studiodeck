A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Glad you asked.

Thoughtful tools should come
with straightforward answers.

Can I use Studiodeck for my own home project?+

Yes. You can start a project yourself as a homeowner, bring together your plans and supplier quotes, and let AI flag possible discrepancies for you to review. Invite your designer, decorator or supplier into the relevant conversations to keep questions, replies and decisions together. The €19 Project Pass, excluding VAT, covers one project owner and one project for 150 days. Invited people can join conversations; the pass includes one person managing the project.

During setup, your workspace is currently called a “studio”. You can name it “Our home refresh” and choose Interior design for a home redecoration project.

Is this only for interior designers?+

No. Studiodeck is built around visual projects, so architects, garden and landscape designers, interior designers, sign makers and multidisciplinary studios can all use it. Bring your drawings and concepts as PDFs or images, and organize the presentation around your work.

How is the checklist different from comments and approvals?+

Comments hold the conversation. Your checklist holds the questions and actions you want to track. Approvals record an explicit decision by a named person. You can turn a comment into a checklist item and link an approval to it. AI discrepancy suggestions are private to your studio. Discussing a finding starts a private conversation; you decide whether to share it with your client. You choose what to share and when work is done.

Do I have to change my design tools?+

Keep creating in the tools you know. Studiodeck accepts PDFs, PowerPoint files, JPG, PNG and WebP images, plus Excel and CSV quotes. Export from your design tool and bring the files into your project. Native CAD and BIM editing are not part of Studiodeck.

What does my client need to get started?+

Just a browser and access to their invited email address. Clients verify their email using a sign-in link, then explore the design, leave feedback and download original files. Access is tied to the invited account and a shared iteration, and can be revoked.

What happens when the design changes?+

Create a new iteration and develop your next idea. Previously shared presentations retain their own files, visuals, budgets and styling, so an old link never silently becomes a new proposal.

What can the automatic AI checks find?+

Possible conflicts in materials, colours, finishes, product models, written dimensions, scope, inclusions and comparable prices. The bathroom specification and supplier quote name different finishes for the same tap. Checks run automatically after uploads finish and when relevant project evidence changes. AI compares relevant evidence within the current iteration and brings possible discrepancies to your private Communication panel with both sources. Inspiration, before photos and unselected alternatives are excluded. AI does not measure drawings from pixels or correct files automatically. Findings stay private for your studio to review, discuss, resolve or dismiss. No findings does not guarantee that every detail is correct.

How do the AI features work?+

All AI features are included in every plan, including the Project Pass. Studiodeck helps organize your files, assemble presentations, flag possible discrepancies across project documents and images, answer budget and scope questions with source references, and refine render realism. Project communication keeps the evidence, follow-up questions and client decisions together. No separate AI activation or add-on purchase is needed. You review the results before sharing.

What counts as an active project?+

One ongoing design commission counts as one project, including all of its presentations, files and revisions. Our proposed plans include 3 active projects for Solo, 15 for Studio and 50 for Practice. Extra active projects cost €10 each per month on all monthly plans; add capacity when you need it. Archive completed work to free a slot; restoring it requires an available slot. Practice includes 15 people, with additional people at €20 each per month. Extra seats do not increase included project capacity. The €19.00 Project Pass covers one project and 150 days of access with a one-time payment. Storage and archive retention terms will be confirmed before launch.

How long does the Project Pass last?+

The €19.00 Project Pass includes one project, one designer and 150 days of project access. It is a one-time payment and does not renew automatically. Extension and file-retention terms will be confirmed before launch.

Can I buy a subscription today?+

These are proposed launch packages, not an active checkout. You can explore a plan and save its details now. Final project quotas and subscription terms will be confirmed before purchasing is enabled.
