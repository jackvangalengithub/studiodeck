Understanding the conversion.

FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT / Illustrative assumptions, not a contract.

Known investment

Construction, glazing, joinery, finishes, furniture, decorative lighting, main bathrooms, design coordination, logistics and a €62,000 client reserve. Base €687,500; premium furniture selection €703,500. Every figure is fictional and uses a uniformly assumed VAT-inclusive basis of 20% solely for demo arithmetic.

Included subquotes

Alpen Bauatelier €248,000 includes Berg Struktur €92,000, Dach & Holz €98,000 and Haus Technik €58,000. Haus Technik includes Lichtwerk Tirol €24,000 and Klima Berg €34,000. Panorama Werk €104,000 includes Alpenglas €64,000. Fir Atelier €78,000 includes Kochraum €16,000. Included subquotes are never added twice.

Optional packages

Wellness €34,800 includes Sauna Form €14,800. The parent begins unselected, so the entire optional scope stays outside the base total. Terrace lounge and wind screens add €12,600. Premium furniture adds €16,000. Selecting all options and premium furniture gives €750,900. Wellness and terrace imagery shows these optional packages.

Conversion boundaries

The design retains the roof silhouette, sound main beams, stone plinth and selected heritage detail. All images, including the imagined original chalet, are generated studies. Before/proposed pairs preserve visual identity but do not establish an existing measured condition. Plans and the 280 m² brief are illustrative and not suitable for construction.

Package interfaces

Structural window enlargement is in Berg Struktur; frames and glazing are in Panorama Werk. Wiring and controls are in Lichtwerk Tirol; decorative light supply is in Lumen Alpin. Kitchen worktops are in Fir Atelier; general floors and wall finishes are in Stein & Faser. The retained tiled stove requires specialist inspection before any real operational proposal.

Unknown and excluded

Concealed timber defects, unexpected ground or rock works, measured surveys, statutory fees, permits, specialist structural/energy reports, property purchase, utility upgrades and extraordinary weather or access disruption are not priced in the known totals. Unknown conditions are not zero-cost items. No compliance, thermal performance or completion-date guarantee is made.

Decision gates

Commission a measured survey and condition assessment; agree the retention and opening-up strategy; review local permissions and specialist designs; approve materials and windows; select options; then freeze procurement packages. The 40-week programme and all supplier lead times are fictional planning assumptions.
