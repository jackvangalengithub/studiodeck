Included in the known investment

Interior construction and installation; kitchen and oak joinery; furniture allowance; decorative lighting; curtains; primary bathroom fixtures; art and styling; design coordination; and a €25,000 client-held contingency. Base €278,500; premium furniture selection €286,500. Amounts use a fictional VAT-inclusive basis.

How subcontractors are counted

Forma Build €82,000 includes Lumen Works €18,500, Flow Systems €14,000 and Terra Surface €21,000. Lumen Works includes Signal Atelier €4,800. Grain Atelier €54,000 includes Quarry Objects €16,000 and Quiet Appliances €12,000. These included amounts are not added again. The optional study library €9,600 is additional to Grain Atelier.

Options and unpriced work

Study library €9,600 and terrace furniture €18,500 are not selected in the base total. Premium furniture adds €8,000. All selections together total €314,600. Landscape design and external works are unknown, not zero, and excluded from every stated total.

Boundaries between packages

Electrical installation and controls are within construction; decorative light fittings are in Halo Objects. Rough-in plumbing is within construction; sanitary fixtures and their installation are in Still Bath. Kitchen worktops are in Quarry Objects; general stone floors are in Terra Surface. The €25,000 contingency is a separate client reserve and is not charged by a supplier.

Not included

Land or property purchase, new-build shell, structural alterations, roof and external glazing, permits, structural engineering, asbestos remediation, boiler replacement, landscaping, external construction, unlisted rooms and unapproved variations.
