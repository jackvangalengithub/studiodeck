WATER & SITE STRATEGY

Make room for the rain.

Coordinate drainage before the garden is built. The concept separates the decorative water feature from rainfall management, with planted collection areas and an optional storage package.

  • Confirm soil, infiltration and groundwater conditions
  • Resolve gradients and discharge routes with specialists
  • Protect existing tree root zones during works
