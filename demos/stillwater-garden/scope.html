Included in the known investment

Landscape construction; pavilion; planting; decorative reflecting pool; lighting; furniture allowance; productive garden; landscape design; first-year establishment care; and an €18,000 client-held contingency. Base selection €213,400; premium furniture €221,400. All figures are fictional and include uniformly assumed 21% VAT for demonstration arithmetic.

Included subquotes

Terra Forma €64,000 includes Ground Atelier €18,000, Raincraft €12,000 and Stone & Line €34,000. Stone & Line includes Quarry North €19,500. Timber Field €32,000 includes Frame Studio €9,000. Included subquotes are never added again.

Options and unknowns

Outdoor kitchen €12,800 is additional to the pavilion. Rainwater storage €6,400 is additional to drainage, even though drainage itself is included in the main contract. Both options are off in the base budget. Premium furniture adds €8,000. All selections together total €240,600. Exceptional or contaminated ground conditions remain unknown and are excluded from every stated total.

Package boundaries

General paths, terraces and lounge masonry are in hardscape. Decorative basin and recirculation equipment are in the water-feature package. Drainage and irrigation distribution are in Raincraft. Furniture covers loose items; the pavilion kitchen is optional. Lighting supply, installation and controls are in Afterglow Gardens. The first-year care allowance is separate from initial planting.

Not included

Property purchase, house construction or renovation, measured surveys, planning or permit fees, specialist structural/electrical/hydraulic engineering, exceptional remediation, utility diversions and unapproved scope changes. Water-feature safety and accessibility must be resolved during technical design. This concept does not make compliance or horticultural suitability claims.

Plans, plants and imagery

The assumed 40 × 35 m plot is illustrative. All four plans are schematic, not surveyed or construction-ready. Plant candidates, quantities, light positions, drainage and levels require site review. All images are AI-generated concepts. The aerial view establishes atmosphere; the masterplan defines conceptual adjacency. The kitchen imagery includes an optional upgrade.
